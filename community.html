AFC TOTTON IN THE COMMUNITY · CHARITY NO. 1206854

We are your club. We are your community.

Over £30,000 raised for local causes in the last year through matchday collections, raffles and auctions.

Work with us

AFC Totton is proud to be at the heart of our local community. AFC Totton in the Community was established to build on that role and make an even greater difference to local people, schools, charities, community groups and organisations.

We have supported a wide range of local charities and fundraising campaigns, welcomed community organisations to matchdays, worked with local schools and provided opportunities for young people to experience football at the Snows Stadium.

Football is our foundation, but our role in the community goes far beyond the pitch.

As a registered charity we can now look at new ways of fundraising and applying for grants to support the development of our youth, pan disability and walking football teams.

Tara Doel, Communities and Grants Manager — communities@afctotton.com
