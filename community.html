The charity arm of AFC Totton

Every matchday is a chance to stand with a local cause.

AFC Totton in the Community holds its own charitable status, enabling the club to apply for grants that support Youth Development, our academies, and our Pan Disability, LGBTQ and Walking Football teams — opening sport to every part of our community.

Through the season we run a charity matchday programme: at home games we welcome a local charity or community group, give them a platform in the ground and online, and share a summary afterwards so supporters always have a resource of local organisations they can turn to if they ever need them.

Away from matchdays, our Community Boards in the clubhouse corridor carry posters and information from local groups, the clubhouse is signed up to the Warm Welcome Campaign as a free warm space for anyone who needs one, and we work with local schools through assemblies and free kids’ cup tickets.

Violence Reduction Partnership

KNIFE CRIME AWARENESS

AFC Totton works with the Hampshire & Isle of Wight Violence Reduction Unit (VRU), part of the Office of the Police and Crime Commissioner, to help reduce knife crime in the New Forest area. The VRU supports the Violence Reduction Partnership — police, health, justice, fire, local authorities, education, prisons and the voluntary and charity sector across Hampshire, the Isle of Wight, Portsmouth and Southampton — working together so that people can live their lives free from violence and the fear of violence.

Twice a year, in May and November, Operation Sceptre (knife crime awareness week) steps up activity across Hampshire to take knives off the streets and educate children, families and communities. The posters, leaflets and resources below were produced with young people from Motive8 — they can be used at any time of year, so please download, print and share them.
