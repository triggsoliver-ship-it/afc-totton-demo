What is walking football?

Created in 2011, walking football keeps the game going for everyone — whatever your age or fitness.

Walking football is a unique amateur sport. It plays much like 5-a-side, but without the physical demands of regular football — and, as the name suggests, there is no running. The main rules:

  • No running or jogging, on or off the ball
  • No physical contact
  • The ball stays below head height
  • No heading
  • No slide tackling
  • No tackling from behind
  • All free-kicks are indirect
  • Defenders stand 3 metres from free-kicks
  • One step only before a free-kick or penalty
  • Shin pads must be worn by all players

Who can play?

Walking football was created to promote the physical and mental well-being of the over-50s, and it has since grown into a brilliant way for people of any age with health conditions — or recovering from injury — to keep playing. At AFC Totton we run an open session with mixed teams of all ages.

Why play?

BODY · MIND · COMMUNITY

Mental health

Exercise helps the body produce dopamine — the happy hormone. Our players report real personal reward and satisfaction from every session.

Physical health

Walking football is proven to improve health — reducing the risk of heart disease and stroke, and helping posture, balance, reactions, blood pressure, cholesterol and type 2 diabetes.

Social life

Social circles can shrink as we get older. Walking football is an active, welcoming community — build friendships and widen your circle.

A sense of purpose

Community is at the heart of AFC Totton. Come and build purpose into your exercise and your week.

Getting started

You may not have kicked a football in years, let alone worn a full kit — don’t worry. Wear whatever you’re comfortable in (within reason): loose-fitting shorts, a t-shirt and trainers are perfect, though a pair of football shoes might give you a competitive edge.

The one thing we insist on is shin pads — an extra layer of protection that keeps the risk of injury down even further.
