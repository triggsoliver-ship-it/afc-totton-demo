AFC TOTTON ALTERNATIVE PROVISION · KEY STAGE 3 & 4

Education, through football.

A sports and education alternative provision with a focus on football — supporting 11–16-year-olds who are struggling in mainstream education to build resilience, confidence and the skills to reintegrate successfully.

Make a referral Email Hannah

About the provision

A learning environment which promotes resilience and well-being.

The AFC Totton Alternative Provision was created to build an environment for 11–16-year-olds to progress within sports education, offering an opportunity for students in Key Stage 3 and 4 who are struggling in mainstream education. We support students who:

  • Have been permanently excluded from school
  • Are unable to attend school due to emotional or health needs
  • Are at risk of disengaging in mainstream education
  • Are at risk of permanent exclusion

Our curriculum has a strong focus on maths and English, giving every student the basic foundations they need to access all other areas of their learning. Our football programme develops students physically, psychologically and socially — promoting teamwork and improving confidence both on and off the pitch.

We have a highly skilled and committed team of staff who create an inclusive environment to support students in regulating their own emotions. Our aim is to equip every student with the skills they need to reintegrate successfully back into mainstream education.

Our curriculum

THE FULL PROGRAMME

Induction

A structured induction programme for all students, including initial assessments.

Timetables

A personalised timetable for every student to support their needs.

Functional English, Maths & ICT

Timetabled lessons to complement the school curriculum.

Physical activity

Timetabled football coaching and games.

Personal development

Understanding the importance of keeping healthy, and supporting future career choices for Key Stage 4 students.

Responsibility

An environment designed to encourage all students to take responsibility for themselves and others.

Key skills

A curriculum designed to support students with the skills they need for later life.

AQA unit awards

Recognised awards that evidence each student’s achievements along the way.

What we commit to

EVERY STUDENT · EVERY SCHOOL
  • Deliver high-quality individualised learning programmes
  • Plan and deliver engaging lessons appropriate to each student’s age, ability, interests and qualification
  • Work in partnership with parents and carers to support the success of each student
  • Adapt learning to cater for a range of learning styles and preferences
  • Provide regular updates on progress through half-termly tracker reports
  • Liaise with the school on any changes to the qualification or course in the best interest of the student
  • Provide daily and weekly registers of attendance
  • Ensure a safe environment for learning
  • Provide qualified and enhanced DBS-checked staff
  • Provide a mentor for each student from the AFC Totton team
