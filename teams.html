A club legend turned manager — Golden Boot winner with 28 goals in the 2022/23 title season and Players’ Player of the Year, passing 50 goals for the Stags within eighteen months of arriving. Now leads the first team and heads up coaching across the club’s academy programmes.

David Wright

Director of Football

More than twenty years at the top of professional recruitment and development: Academy Director at Stoke City, Head of Recruitment at Portsmouth, Academy Recruitment Manager at Brighton & Hove Albion and national youth recruitment for Southampton.

Walking Football

Keeping the game going, whatever the age.

Our walking football group meets every Tuesday and Friday morning, 10:00–11:30, on the enclosed all-weather MUGA at the Snows Stadium. The session is aimed at the over-50s but is open to all — most of the group are in their late 60s to mid 70s, and new faces are always welcome.

The rules keep it safe and social: no running, no physical contact, ball below head height, no heading, no slide tackles — and shin pads on. The benefits go well beyond fitness: regular exercise, new friendships and a reason to get out of the house twice a week.

The group even includes a piece of football history — Louise Gardner, one of the “Lost Lionesses”, who played for Southampton Ladies and represented England at the first Women’s World Cup in Mexico in 1971.

Pan Disability

Football for everyone.

Our Pan Disability team is run in partnership with Dimensions, the not-for-profit organisation supporting people with learning disabilities, autism and complex needs. The team plays in the Pan Disability League — founded by the Saints Foundation and run this season by Eastleigh FC — where teams are graded by disability level so everyone competes on a fair footing.

Matchdays are monthly, with three matches per matchday and eighteen competitive games across the six-month season. Training is every Wednesday at 10:00am on the MUGA at the Snows Stadium.

The programme is run by Ian Knight and Gillian Cousins of Dimensions alongside Dave Penny, the club’s Vice-Chairman and Community Manager.

SAINTS AT THE SNOWS

The Snows Stadium is also a home for the professional game: Southampton FC B play their Premier League 2 home fixtures here, and Southampton FC Women call the Snows home in the FA Women’s National League — bringing the next generation of Saints, and some of the best women’s football in the region, to Totton.
