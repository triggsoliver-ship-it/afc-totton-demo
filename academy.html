About the Academy

Two years. A Level 3 qualification. A pathway into the game.

The AFC Totton Academy is our full-time programme for 16 to 19-year-old players: nationally recognised BTEC Level 3 study combined with structured football development, all based at the Snows Stadium. Scholarship places are available through our partnership with Access Sport College.

Players train and play in club colours with a genuine pathway in front of them — from the Academy into the Under-23s and, for the very best, Scott Rendell’s first-team squad. The academy side were league champions in 2025/26.

Off the pitch, the qualifications open doors of their own: university in the UK, football scholarships in the United States, or straight into employment in sport and fitness.

Our courses

BTEC LEVEL 3 · TWO YEARS

BTEC Level 3 Extended Diploma in Sport Development & Coaching

Entry: five GCSEs at grade 4–9

A two-year, 100% coursework qualification for players who want to understand the game from the inside — coaching, development and the sport industry — while training daily.

  • No exams — assessed entirely through coursework across both years
  • Coaching practice delivered at a working National League South club
  • Progression to university, US football scholarships or employment in sport

BTEC Level 3 Extended Diploma in Sport, Fitness & Personal Training

Entry: four GCSEs including English & Maths

A two-year route into the fitness industry, combining the Extended Diploma with CIMSPA-aligned gym instructing and personal training qualifications.

  • Industry work placements built into the course
  • Leave qualified to work as a gym instructor and personal trainer
  • Progression to university, higher study or straight into the industry
