The 1886 · restaurant & bar

OPEN TO EVERYONE · NO MEMBERSHIP NEEDED

Between the main stand and the club shop you’ll find The 1886 — the club’s own restaurant and bar, open Monday to Saturday, 9:00am–2:00pm for breakfast and lunch, run by Caroline Keats with Brenda Andrews and Angie Hales.

On matchdays the kitchen turns out pies, burgers, chips and a proper Bovril; through the season look out for Sunday roasts and three-course lunch competitions on the club’s Facebook page. Event catering is available for 2 to 120 guests across all three suites, with buffet and event menus tailored to your occasion.
