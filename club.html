A brief history of The Stags

AFC Totton came into existence through the amalgamation of Totton FC and Totton Athletic in 1974/75, with the club's lineage running back to 1886. The Stags started life in Hampshire League Division Two, winning promotion to Division One in 1979/80.

The 1981/82 season remains long in the memory: Trevor Parker's side won the Hampshire League Division One title, the Russell-Cotes Cup, the Southampton Senior Cup, the Echo Trophy, the Reg Mathieson Trophy and the Hampshire Intermediate Cup.

In 2006/07 the club reached the FA Vase final at Wembley in front of a record crowd of 36,232, with 17,000 supporters travelling from the town.

Totton won the Wessex Premier League in 2007/08 on an incredible 106 points, securing promotion to the Southern League for the first time in their history. The Hampshire Senior Cup followed in 2009/10 and 2010/11, alongside a run to the FA Cup Second Round Proper.

Under Jimmy Ball the club were crowned Southern League Division One South champions in 2022/23, completing a remarkable treble with the Champion of Champions Super Cup and a third consecutive Southampton Senior Cup.

In 2024/25, in front of a packed Snows Stadium, Ethan Taylor's 70th-minute chip beat Gloucester City 1-0 in the play-off final to secure promotion to the National League South — the highest level the club has ever played at. The Stags finished 12th in their debut Step 2 campaign.

The Snows Stadium

From the M27

Leave at Junction 2 and follow the A326, then the A36 Salisbury Road towards Totton. The ground is on Mally's Way, SO40 2DE.

From Southampton

Take the A33 towards Redbridge, then the A36 into Totton. Find us on what3words at ///comments.reflector.livid.

Parking

Matchday parking at the ground is limited. Free parking is available on the Calmore Industrial Estate, Brunel Road, half a mile past the ground. Eight accessible bays on site — contact the club ahead of your visit for assistance.

Facilities

Bars, food kiosks, the 1886 restaurant, IPS Suite hospitality and the club shop, open on matchdays. Call 023 8086 8981.

Ground regulations

  • Entering the field of play is strictly forbidden — pitch encroachers will be arrested and may be prosecuted.
  • No ball games in the spectator areas during the match.
  • Drums and musical instruments are permitted — please be considerate to those around you.
  • Obscene, abusive or racist language will not be tolerated.
  • Alcohol in glasses may only be consumed inside the Clubhouse — plastic containers only outside.
  • Climbing floodlights, fences or stands is prohibited.
  • Do not stand in hatched or designated safety areas.
  • No bottles, cans, glass, smoke bombs or flares — and no throwing of any objects.
  • Vehicles are parked at the owner's risk.
  • The club reserves the right to refuse admission or eject any person breaching these regulations.
  • Only food and drink purchased at the Snows Stadium may be consumed inside the ground.
  • Friendly dogs are welcome on leads.
  • Breaches may lead to ejection from the ground and prosecution.
