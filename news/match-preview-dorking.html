MATCH PREVIEW · 17 August 2026 · BY THE STAGS MEDIA TEAM

Stags host Dorking Wanderers chasing three from three

Scott Rendell's side return to the Snows Stadium on Tuesday evening for their third fixture of the 2026/27 campaign.

The Stags return to the Snows Stadium on Tuesday evening for their third fixture of the 2026/27 Enterprise National League South campaign, with Dorking Wanderers the visitors.

Two clean sheets and six points from the opening two games provide the perfect platform.

Scott Rendell's side have made an excellent start to the new season, opening their campaign with a 1-0 victory away at Farnham Town before recording another 1-0 win over Billericay Town at the Snows Stadium on Saturday. Two clean sheets and six points from the opening two games provide the perfect platform as the Stags look to make it three wins from three.

Dorking will arrive looking for their first victory of the campaign. The Wanderers began their season with a narrow 1-0 defeat at home to Dagenham & Redbridge before picking up their first point with a goalless draw away at Maidenhead United.

The two sides know each other well, having met twice during the 2025/26 season. Dorking claimed a 2-1 victory at Meadowbank in September, with Tony Lee putting the Stags ahead before Alfie Rutherford and Charlie Carter turned the game around for the hosts.

The return fixture at the Snows Stadium in April produced a different outcome, as Totton came from behind to secure a 2-1 victory. Alfie Merritt and Christie Ward were on target in the second half to complete the comeback.

Tuesday's fixture provides another opportunity for Totton to build momentum and make home advantage count.
