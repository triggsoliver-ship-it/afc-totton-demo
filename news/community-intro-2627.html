COMMUNITY · 15 August 2026 · BY THE STAGS MEDIA TEAM

We are your club. We are your community.

AFC Totton in the Community begins a new season with more than £30,000 raised for local causes in the last year alone.

As we welcome everyone back to the Snows Stadium for another season in the National League South, there is plenty to look forward to on the pitch, but there is also lots happening away from the football too.

Football is our foundation, but our role in the community goes far beyond the pitch.

AFC Totton is proud to be at the heart of our local community, and AFC Totton in the Community (Charity No. 1206854) was established to build on that role and make an even greater difference to local people, schools, charities, community groups and organisations.

Over the past few seasons we have supported a wide range of local charities and fundraising campaigns, welcomed community organisations to matchdays, worked with local schools and provided opportunities for young people to experience football at the Snows Stadium.

We have also linked with our Youth Teams, Pan Disability, Walking Football and Beach Soccer teams, to start making football accessible to more people within our community.

In the last year, with matchday collections and prizes given for raffles and auctions, we have helped raise over £30,000 for the local community.

If you are involved with a local charity, school, community group or fundraising event and would like to work with AFC Totton in the Community, please get in touch.
